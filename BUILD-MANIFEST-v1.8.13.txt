Privacy boundary:
The app sends only a random installation ID, version, platform, an
allow-listed event name, and safe metadata. The backend stores a one-way HMAC
of the installation ID. Provider credentials, portal URLs, MAC addresses,
PINs, stream URLs, channel names, raw IP addresses, and personal files are
not part of the analytics payload.
