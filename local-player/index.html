About, FAQ & Policies

STB PLAY is a player application. It does not sell, provide, host, or distribute IPTV subscriptions or media.

What does STB PLAY provide?

Only the player software. You must use a portal and content source that you are authorised to access.

Why is a channel or movie not playing?

Playback depends on your provider, internet connection, stream format, and server availability. Try another quality or VLC when available, then contact your authorised provider.

What if I forget the parental PIN?

Use the saved 8-digit recovery code from Settings. A new recovery code is issued after recovery. STB PLAY does not contain a shared master PIN.

Privacy policy

Portal details, MAC address, preferences, favourites, and watch progress are stored locally on this device. Diagnostics are opt-in: collection starts only after you press Start fresh test. Reports exclude your MAC address, PIN, tokens, cookies, provider hostname, channel/title names, and full stream links. STB PLAY does not intentionally sell personal data.

Terms and acceptable use

You are responsible for using lawful, authorised portals and content. Do not use STB PLAY to access, copy, redistribute, or share content without permission. The software is provided as-is and availability is not guaranteed.

Application updatesCheck whether a newer STB PLAY version is available.

Playback diagnostic

Diagnostics are off by default. Press Start fresh test only when support asks. Reproduce the problem, download the report, and attach the JSON file to your support message. It records playback timing and error types, not your MAC, PIN, tokens, cookies, provider hostname, or full stream links.
